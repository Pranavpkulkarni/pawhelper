About Paw Helper

Started With One Rescued Pup. Now a Movement.

Founded in 2022 in Latur, Maharashtra, Paw Helper began with a single injured street dog and the resolve to do more. Today we are a registered NGO running daily rescue, medical and adoption programs across the district.

Fully Transparent Funding

Every rupee documented. Audited annual reports are publicly available and we are audit-ready for corporate CSR compliance.

Ground-Level Community Work

200+ trained volunteers, municipal partnerships and school outreach programs build lasting change — not just charity.

Professional Veterinary Network

Collaboration with licensed vets for surgeries, vaccinations and long-term rehabilitation of every rescued animal.
